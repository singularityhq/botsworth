# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
By taking part, you agree to follow it. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful, accept criticism of your work without taking it as an attack, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code, use GitHub's private reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and
choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say that the report is about conduct. Only the maintainers can see it.

Maintainers who receive a report keep the reporter's identity private and decide the response, from a private
warning to removal from the project, following the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
